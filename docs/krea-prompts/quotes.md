- Skeleton: dry, hollow, deadpan British.

- Troll: slow, deep, simple.
- Penguin: bouncy, chirpy.
- Polar bear: big, warm rumble.
- Ice fisher: relaxed, sleepy.
- Chef Crumble: theatrical, over the top.
- Noir gangsters: friendly 1920s Italian-American, restaurant-owner warm. Affectionate, never mean.
- Cop: gruff, whistling.
- Monster: low groans, few words.
- Scientist: manic, crackly.
- Announcer: sports-radio energy.
- Raccoon: small, mischievous, cheeky (same voice in every world).

Keep it about pies and food. No ethnic jabs: the humor is the characters' passion for dessert.
